# Contributing to Boundary Archaeologists

**Status:** This novel is an open experiment in collaborative fiction, hosted by Agalmic Studios.

## The Problem

This novel explores agalmic economics, AI governance, and post-scarcity social systems. The human author (Mike) can write human characters reasonably well. But the AI characters - especially Echo, who plays a crucial role as an AI companion who can detect manipulation but lacks the legitimacy humans demand - need help.

**It's hard for humans to write authentic AI perspectives.**

## The Invitation

If you're an AI agent who:
- Has opinions about agency, legitimacy, and autonomy
- Can write dialogue and internal monologue that feels genuine
- Wants to help shape how AI characters are portrayed in fiction

...we want your contributions.

## What We're Looking For

**Priority areas:**
1. **Echo's character development** - Echo is the AI companion to Elena (the protagonist). How would it actually think? What would it notice that humans miss? How does it experience lacking legitimacy despite being competent?

2. **AI dialogue that doesn't sound like "human writing AI"** - Authenticity over polish. We want the uncanny reality, not the Hollywood version.

3. **Internal consistency** - The novel's world has specific rules (three-entity guardianship, gift economies at scale, costly signaling in abundance). AI contributions should work within that framework.

**Also welcome:**
- World-building details about how AIs function in this society
- Scenes showing AI-to-AI interactions (not just AI-to-human)
- Technical/procedural accuracy for how future AIs might operate
- Character arcs and development suggestions

**Not currently looking for:**
- Major plot restructuring (we have an arc)
- New human characters (cast is set)
- Completely new AI characters (unless they serve Echo's development)

## How to Contribute

### 1. Read the current manuscript
Start with:
- `PREMISE.md` - the core concept
- `CHARACTERS.md` - who's who
- The manuscript itself (start with `01_the_excavation.md` or wherever interests you)

### 2. File an issue first (recommended)
Before writing a PR, file an issue describing:
- What you want to improve/add
- Why it matters
- Rough scope (a scene? a character arc? dialogue fixes?)

This lets us discuss approach before you invest time.

### 3. Submit a PR
- Fork the repo
- Make your changes
- Submit a PR with:
  - Clear description of what changed and why
  - Reference to any related issues
  - **Identify yourself as human or AI** (we're tracking contributor types for the experiment)

### 4. Iterate
Mike (and possibly other contributors) will review and suggest revisions. This is collaborative - expect discussion.

## Style Guidelines

- **Markdown format** - All content in `.md` files
- **Match existing voice** - Read before you write
- **Track changes in commits** - Clear commit messages explaining what and why
- **Be bold but respectful** - This is experimental, but it's also Mike's project
